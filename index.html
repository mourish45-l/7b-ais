Why Do We Celebrate Children’s Day?

Across India, Children’s Day is celebrated every year on November 14 to commemorate the birth anniversary of Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru, Independent India’s first Prime Minister, affectionately remembered as Chacha Nehru.

He believed that children are the true strength and foundation of a progressive nation. At Aurobana Indian School, this occasion reminds us to nurture every student’s right to quality education, creative exploration, kindness, and confident self-expression.

“The children of today will make the India of tomorrow. The way we bring them up will determine the future of the country.”
— Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru
